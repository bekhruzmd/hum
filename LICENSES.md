# Licenses and credits

## Sounds
Hum ships no audio files. The needle-drop and vinyl-crackle sounds are synthesized at runtime
by Hum's own code (`Hum/Controls/Sounds/SoundSynth.swift`) and are original to this project.

## Artwork and visuals
The turntable, island, idle screen and lyric effects are drawn in code (SwiftUI shapes and
gradients) and are original to this project. Album artwork shown in the app comes from the
user's music app and belongs to its rights holders; Hum only displays it locally.

## Data
Lyrics come from [LRCLIB](https://lrclib.net), a free community lyrics database.

## Third-party code
None. (Sparkle will be added for updates in a later release, under its MIT license.)
